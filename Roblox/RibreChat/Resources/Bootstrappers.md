## Roblox Bootstrappers

- https://github.com/bloxstraplabs/bloxstrap
    - https://fishstrap.com/
        - Fork of Bloxstrap
    - https://quickstrap.xyz/
        - Another fork of Bloxstrap
    - https://github.com/Cristiano100/roforge
        - Legacy/older fork of Bloxstrap

- https://github.com/equinoxhq/lucem
    - Bootstrapper for Sober (Roblox on Linux), similar to Bloxstrap
    - Written in Nim
- https://github.com/idkncc/rsblox
    - Minimalistic game launcher for Roblox, written in Rust

- https://github.com/Wookhq/Lution-old
    - Literally a Bloxstrap clone for Linux
